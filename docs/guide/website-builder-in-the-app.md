# The website builder in the Educo app

The Educo app on a phone or a tablet has the website builder inside it. It is the same builder as on a computer: the
pages you edit in the app are the pages you edit in a browser, and everything in
[the layout story](./layout-story.md) and [the reference guide](./website-builder.md) applies.

## Opening it

Mrs Mensah opens the Educo app on her phone and taps **More**, then **Website builder**. The builder fills the screen. At
the top is a **Back** arrow and the words "Website builder"; the app's tab bar steps out of the way while she edits.

The builder lays itself out for the screen it is on:

| Where | What you get |
|---|---|
| A phone | The phone editor: one row of buttons at the top, **More** for everything else, the blocks **+** bottom-right |
| A tablet held upright | The same one-row bar; the page drawn at the tablet's own width |
| A tablet held sideways (1024 or wider) | The full editor, as on a computer |

Turning a tablet re-lays the buttons. It never changes the page.

The builder wears the app's theme. Choose **More → Theme** (Light, Dark, Midnight or Purple) and the builder's buttons,
panels and the bar at the top follow it. The page itself keeps the website's own theme, because that is what visitors see.

## Back

Android's **Back** (the button, or the swipe from the side) works the way you expect:

1. If the builder has something open, such as its own **More** panel, Back closes it and you keep editing.
2. When there is nothing left to close, Back takes you out to the app's **More** tab.

Back never closes the app in the middle of an edit. On an iPhone or iPad, use the **Back** arrow at the top; the swipe
from the edge is switched off in the builder, so a drag near the edge of the page moves your block instead of leaving.

**A finger near the edge.** The thin strip down each side of a phone's screen belongs to the phone: a swipe that starts
there means Back. So when a block reaches that strip, the builder doesn't draw the handles that would sit in it. Resize the
block from its other side, or set its size and **Columns** in the Inspector.

## Your work is saved as you go

The builder saves on the device while you type. Pause for a moment and your words are kept, even if the app is closed in
the background or the phone runs short of memory. If the page inside the builder stops, the builder starts it again
and your work is still there.

There is one small gap. Words typed in the last second or so before the app is forced shut can be lost. Pausing for a
moment before leaving the app is enough.

## Photos from your phone

Add an **Image** block and tap **Upload**. Your phone's photo picker opens. Choose a photo and it lands on the page,
already made smaller so that the page stays light on data.

## When there is no signal

Open the builder without a connection and it opens the copy saved on this device, with a yellow notice: *"You are
offline. This is the saved copy; your changes save on this device."* Keep editing. When the signal is back, tap
**Try again** and the builder goes live with your changes.

## Links

- A link to another website opens in the phone's browser. The builder stays where it was.
- A link to `educo://fees`, `educo://messages` or `educo://reports` opens that screen of the app.

## Opening app screens from a link

These addresses open the app on the right screen, whether it was running or not:

| Address | Opens |
|---|---|
| `educo://fees` | Fees |
| `educo://messages` | Messages (Chat) |
| `educo://reports` | Reports |
| `educo://site-editor` | The website builder |

## Not yet

- **Signing in.** The builder in the app does not ask who you are yet. It will, once Educo has accounts. Until then your
  pages are kept on the device you edit them on.
