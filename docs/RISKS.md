# The risk register (RULE RK)

Opened 2026-09-28 at the user's request: the two risks that could turn a one-of-a-kind builder into an ordinary one, each with
its mitigation as a rule in `CLAUDE.md`, and the measurement that shows the mitigation is working. Re-read at the start of
every area of work; a new risk is added here with its rule the day it is found.

| # | Risk | Why it is real | Mitigation (a rule, not a hope) | Measured by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Scope.** Layout, components, semantics, documentation, data budgets, offline, payments, languages, templates: years of work if built as features. | Every one of them is right; together they can stall the product before the first school uses it. | One area at a time, measured clean before the next (a tier, a component, a language). Work in progress is one area. Every area ships something a real school can use the day it closes. Every rule has a measurement or it is not done. | The ledger of the current area reads fixed or decided before the next starts; the demo site shows the area; the sweep numbers of that area are in the report. |
| R2 | **Distribution.** A better builder does not spread by itself where customers are not looking for a builder. | The institutions that need it (schools, clinics, churches, small businesses, local government) buy from people they know, not from a landing page. | A pilot cohort of ten real schools (Nigeria and Ghana first) recruited while the components are built; their feedback in the ledger, their pages in the sweep. Distribution planned as product work with an owner and a date, through school-management vendors, parent groups, church and mosque networks, local government; a one-hour "show it once" path. | Sites published by the cohort, pages kept up to date, a second person editing; reported beside the sweep numbers. |
| R3 | **Watering down the rare parts.** The engine proven against real pages, the block-model AI, developing countries first — each is easy to shortcut under time pressure. | They are what makes the product different; without them it is one more builder. | Non-negotiable: a change that weakens one is a bug in the ledger, not a trade-off. | The sweeps still run on every change; the LLM builder's output is validated as a block tree, never parsed HTML; RULE AF's numbers are in every page report. |
| R4 | **Documentation written at the end.** | Everything after the layout reads it; written late it is wrong and unread. | RULE L: the story grows with every finished area, in the same change. RULE DOC: one format (Docusaurus), one source. | A finished area with no story is not finished (the rules test and the PR checklist). |
| R5 | **The harness lying.** A sweep that passes for the wrong reason is worse than none. | Measured this week: six false positives and one false negative in the audit before the engine's real bugs surfaced. | A bug in a test is a bug (rule 8); every finding is probed through the UI before it is called an engine bug; the sweep report names what it covered. | The ledger's NOT-A-BUG lines carry the measurement; the harness has its own tests. |
